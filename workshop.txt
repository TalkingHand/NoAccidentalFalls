title=[42.22.2] No Accidental Falls
description=One step too many on a balcony or a roof should not kill your character. No Accidental Falls stops you at the edge of a floor over open air, as if there were a wall there, so you no longer walk off a balcony without a railing, a roof edge or an unfinished upper floor by accident.
description=
description=[b]Stairs, ladders, sheet ropes, windows and fence climbs work as before.[/b]
description=
description=[h2]Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]No walking off edges:[/b] where the floor ends over open air, walking, running and sprinting all stop at the edge. You can still walk along the edge.
description=[*][b]Climbs onto a drop (optional):[/b] vanilla already refuses running vaults and wall climbs onto a drop. A deliberate climb (holding E at a railing, or [b]Climb over[/b] / [b]Climb through[/b] from the right-click menu) still goes over by default. Turn off [b]Allow deliberate climbs over a drop[/b] to refuse those too; your character then says "Too high to climb down here". Windows and railings with a sheet rope stay allowed.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Sandbox options[/h2]
description=[table]
description=[tr][th]Option[/th][th]Default[/th][th]Description[/th][/tr]
description=[tr][td]Block walking off edges[/td][td]On[/td][td]Walking or running into open air over a drop stops you at the edge.[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]Guard climbs over a drop[/td][td]On[/td][td]Checks climbs over low fences and railings and through windows. It only has an effect while the next option is off.[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]Allow deliberate climbs over a drop[/td][td]On[/td][td]On: deliberate climbs still go over a drop. Off: they are refused too, unless the window or railing has a sheet rope.[/td][/tr]
description=[/table]
description=
description=[h2]Limits[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Your character is kept 0.25 tiles back from an open-air edge (0.1 at the top of a ladder), so the fall normally never starts.
description=[*]Other falls, such as through a collapsing floor, are not prevented.
description=[*]Tested in single player with real key presses, ladders, stairs, upper-floor windows and sheet ropes. Gamepad and multiplayer are not tested yet.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Lua only, runs on the client. No vanilla files are replaced.
description=[*]Safe to add to or remove from an existing save.
description=[/list]
